## The thesis

Most AI lives on screens. I’m building intelligence for the world of **drawings, parts, machines, inspection, suppliers, and industrial capacity**.

The goal is not another dashboard. It is a reasoning layer that can move from incomplete product information to production-ready decisions — with evidence, traceability, and physics kept in the loop.

| | Focus | Direction |
